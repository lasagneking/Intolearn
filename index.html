How this works

Ingredients are compared with symptom days in your local diary, counting a symptom logged either the same day or the next day. Ingredients logged on almost every day, or without enough repeats, are left out — only ones noticeably above your own baseline symptom rate are shown. The more consistently you log, the more useful this becomes.
